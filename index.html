Clinico-genomic data at population scale

See the genome and the clinic together.

GenoSphere is Helix's real-world clinico-genomic dataset: clinical-grade exomes from more than 560,000 consented participants, linked to their longitudinal electronic health records.

Clinico-genomic data pairs each person's genome with their real-world clinical journey, the diagnoses, lab trends, medications, and notes that accumulate over years, so you can move from a genetic signal to its clinical consequence inside a single dataset.

What GenoSphere is

Clinico-genomic data, four layers deep.

GenoSphere links each participant's genome to the real-world evidence that surrounds it. Together they form a clinico-genomic record: genetics read alongside the clinical life it plays out in.

Genomics
Clinical-grade exome across 6,000 genes.
The longitudinal record

Clinical data, linked over time.

Every HRN participant carries the potential for linked health records across four kinds of clinical data. Depth varies by participant.

Genome meets clinic

Pathogenic variants, attached to real patients.

The value is in the link. For each gene, GenoSphere holds the participants carrying pathogenic or likely-pathogenic variants and the clinical cohort they map to. Pathogenicity follows non-conflicting ClinVar assertions. Figures as of 15 May 2026.
